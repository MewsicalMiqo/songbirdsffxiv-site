Our story

It started in 2017, when Performance Mode finally gave bards a real instrument to play. Since our 2018 debut, the Songbirds have grown from a small circle of voices into an ensemble that performs live concerts across every server and datacenter — players from all over the world filling Limsa Lominsa Lower Decks, dancing, cheering and waving their glow sticks, while hundreds more watch on Twitch.

Behind every performance is a mountain of work most people never see. As Mewsical Miqo told TheGamer, the band had to "learn a wide range of disparate skills, some of which I had little to no existing proficiency in" — the PC hardware that runs a full octet of accounts, the custom software that drives our shows, the low-level research into how in-game music actually works. And the arrangements are composed in dedicated music production software: some come together in fifteen minutes to an hour, others take far, far longer.

Co-manager Raven Ambree says the response from fans is "overwhelming wholesomeness and support" — people messaging to say a concert made their terrible day better, that we brought them happiness. For us, that's the best review a stage can get.

And there's a stage for every kind of moment: in-game weddings, entertainment venues, Free Company recruitment drives, festival appearances and our regular pop-up concerts — with the latest expansion songs always finding their way into the setlist.

Featured in TheGamer's Final Fantasy XIV community spotlight, where we were called "an idol group that perform concerts across all servers and data centers."

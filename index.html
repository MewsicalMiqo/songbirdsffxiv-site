About the Songbirds

A crew of adorable Miqo'tes with a passion for music.

The Songbirds were created to test the limits of bard performance and we grew along-side the players who fell in love with our music. Today we strive to keep the dream alive, bringing our songs to every corner of Eorzea and beyond.

Our craft is a labor of love, driven by the support of our incredible fans.

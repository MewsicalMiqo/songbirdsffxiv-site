How it works

We just need three things from you.

  1. 01

    Join our Discord community

    Our Discord server is where you go to book events, ask questions, and connect with other fans.

  2. 02

    Submit your request

    Go to the #songbirds-request channel and use the Appy form and fill out all the necessary information. Feel free to send me MewsicalMiqo a DM or ping if you have any questions.

  3. 03

    We perform

    Once your request is accepted. You just need to show up to your event! You bring the crowd; we bring the songs.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

How far in advance should we book?+

Give us as much lead time as you can, a week is ideal, but most of the time we can be flexible. Give us the time and date and we'll do our best to accommodate your request.

Can I request specific songs?+

You can certainly pick out specific songs from our repertoire. If you prefer to be more hands-off, just let me know the vibe/genre and I'll DJ it up, or we can let XIV/Twitch chat take the wheel!

Which datacenters/locations do you cover?+

All of them (except China). With datacenter travel our band can visit any server. As for location, we prefer to perform in safe areas accessible to the public. Unfortunately that means we cannot accept requests to perform in wilderness or inside the Sanctum of the Twelve.

How much does it cost to book you?+

I am semi-retired from playing FFXIV so I am not currently accepting gil payments. I will gladly perform for your event at our legacy rate of $15USD/hour. This helps me cover the upkeep costs of maintaining the band.
